What we set out to learn

Our theme: understand the status, benefits and challenges of inland fisheries in Mypadu, Nellore district, and encourage sustainable fish farming.

  • Fish speciesWhich fish are raised or caught locally.
  • MethodsHow ponds are stocked, fed and harvested.
  • LivelihoodWhat fish farming adds to a family's income.
  • ChallengesFeed cost, water quality, disease and market prices.
  • SchemesWhich government support farmers know about and use.
  • SustainabilityPractices that keep ponds healthy for the long term.
